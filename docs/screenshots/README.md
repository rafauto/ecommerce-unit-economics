# Screenshots

`demo.gif` is used at the top of the main README. Recording script (about 20 seconds):

1. Open the app with the sample store. Pause on the KPI row and the yellow verdict
   ("97% of break-even").
2. In the sidebar, move **Price change** to +5%. The verdict turns green, and the deltas appear.
3. Open the **Lever impact** tab to show that price is the strongest lever.
4. Open the **CAC by channel** tab to show influencers at about R$ 61 per customer.

Tips: use a 1280×720 browser window and light theme, record at 10–15 fps, and keep the file
under 5 MB (e.g. with ScreenToGif or Kap).
